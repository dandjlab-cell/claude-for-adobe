# How to use me

Click any folder or clips in Premiere and I can see them. Then tell me what you want done.

I can transcribe the footage, hear where the talking is, tell the talking head from the b-roll, and cut it tight for social. I can organize your files into bins, make a sequence from a folder, show you a frame, or answer "what's said and when".

Everything happens on a duplicate sequence. Cmd+Z undoes each step. Edits that change the project wait for your click.

The Cut silences button at the top works on its own, no Claude needed.
